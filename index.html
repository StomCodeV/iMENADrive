Curriculum

What's inside the course

Twelve levels covering everything from your first time behind the wheel to the theory and hazard awareness you'll need on the road.

Twelve structured levels

Start with the fundamentals of driving and work up to complex intersections, night driving, and hazard recognition. Each level unlocks when you pass the one before it — no skipping ahead.

Built around Rwandan roads

Lessons use the situations you'll actually meet: the Kimironko roundabout at rush hour, the hills around Nyamirambo, moto taxis weaving through Remera, school crossings in Kicukiro.

Kinyarwanda and English

Every lesson, question, and road sign is available in both languages. Switch at any time without losing your progress or place in the course.

Practice that adapts to you

The platform tracks which questions you get wrong and serves you more of those topics until you've mastered them. No time wasted on things you already know.

Full mock examinations

Timed practice exams modelled on the official process. Detailed scoring and a complete review of every question, so you know exactly where you stand.

Certificate on completion

Finish all twelve levels and receive an iMENA Drive certificate with a unique verification code. It documents what you've studied before your official examination.

How it works

Six stages, from first lesson to exam ready

The course is built as a path — each stage prepares you for the next, and your progress is visible at every step.

01

Study the basics

Road signs, road markings, and the rules that govern who goes first. Illustrated lessons in your language.

02

Practise as you go

Questions after every lesson. Get them wrong, and the platform brings them back until you've got them right.

03

Work through scenarios

Realistic road situations where you decide what to do and see why the safest answer is the safest.

04

Take mock exams

Timed practice exams that mirror the official format. Complete review after every question.

05

Fix your weak spots

Your dashboard shows which topics need more work and generates targeted practice for them.

06

Sit the official exam

When your mock scores are consistently strong, you're ready to book your official examination and continue practical training with an instructor.

About

Why we built this

iMENA Drive is made by iMENA Hub, a small technology studio in Kigali. We built it because there wasn't a good digital resource for learning to drive in Rwanda — the material that existed was either out of date, only in English, or spread across WhatsApp groups and PDF files.

This is our attempt to fix that. It's free, it's in both of Rwanda's main languages, and it's designed to actually teach the road rather than just help you memorise answers. If you find something wrong or want to help improve it, get in touch.
