Important

What iMENA Drive is - and what it is not.

iMENA Drive is an educational preparation platform. It prepares you for the official driving examination process. It is not the official government examination, and it does not issue a driving licence.

Practical driving must be completed with a qualified instructor and through the authorised Rwandan process.
